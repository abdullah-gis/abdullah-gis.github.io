Conference presentations, research talks, and academic engagements related to GIS, remote sensing, groundwater assessment, and environmental analysis.

International Conference / Oral Presentation
Nexus of Climate Change and DRR: Anticipatory Actions

Delivered an oral presentation titled “A Comparative Study of Groundwater Quality Using GIS-Based Techniques for Towns of Lahore” at the International Conference on “Nexus of Climate Change and DRR: Anticipatory Actions.”

The research presented a comparative assessment of groundwater quality across different towns of Lahore using GIS-based spatial-analysis techniques. The study focused on the spatial distribution and comparison of groundwater-quality conditions and demonstrated the role of GIS in identifying areas of concern and supporting environmental monitoring and decision-making.

Date: August 01–03, 2025

Venue: Baragali Summer Campus, University of Peshawar, Pakistan

Organizer: Department of Geography and Geomatics, University of Peshawar

Presentation Type: Oral Research Presentation
